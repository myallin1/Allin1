Privacy Policy — Allin1

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Allin1 ("we", "our", "the app") is operated by NJ Tech for the Erode, Tamil Nadu super-app platform, covering the Customer, Hero (delivery/rider partner), Seller, and Admin apps. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account info: name, phone number, email address used to sign in.
  • Location: your device's location while the app is open, used to show nearby sellers/riders, set pickup/drop points, and track active rides or deliveries. We do not collect location in the background when the app is closed.
  • Identity verification documents (SOS feature only): Aadhaar number, PAN number, driving license number, and photos of these documents, collected only if you choose to verify your identity for the SOS emergency-help feature. This is optional and used solely to confirm identity for safety verification.
  • Payment & wallet data: order/ride amounts, wallet coin balance, and transaction history for services booked through the app. We do not store your card or bank details — payments are processed through UPI/payment partners.
  • Photos: images you choose to upload (e.g. KYC documents, food/grocery order photos, seller product photos).
  • Device/usage data: app version, crash logs, and basic analytics to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core services: bike-taxi rides, food/grocery ordering, seller marketplace listings, and SOS emergency assistance.
  • To verify identity for SOS/safety features, reviewed manually by our admin team.
  • To process payments and maintain your in-app wallet balance and transaction history.
  • To send you order/ride status notifications.
  • To improve app reliability (crash reports, performance monitoring).

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Location and contact details are shared only with the hero/rider or seller assigned to your specific order, solely to complete that order. Identity verification documents are visible only to our admin approval team and are never shared with third parties except where required by law.

Data retention & deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account, or by contacting us using the details below. Identity verification documents are deleted upon account deletion unless retention is legally required.

Your choices

  • Location access can be revoked any time from your device's app permission settings — core booking features that require a pickup point will ask you to enable it again when needed.
  • SOS identity verification is entirely optional.
  • You may delete your account at any time from the Settings screen.

Children's privacy

Allin1 is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact us

For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at support@myallin1.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
